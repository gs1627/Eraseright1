POLICY 1: CUSTOMER CONTACT DATA

Customer email addresses and phone numbers should be
deleted when a verified erasure request is approved,
unless a documented legal retention requirement applies.

POLICY 2: SHIPPING INFORMATION

Shipping addresses should be deleted after fulfilment
when they are no longer required for an active order
or documented legal obligation.

POLICY 3: FINANCIAL RECORDS

Invoices and transaction records may need to be retained
for the applicable statutory retention period.
Such records must not be automatically deleted.
Where possible, unnecessary personal information should
be anonymised while preserving required financial details.

POLICY 4: SUPPORT RECORDS

Support tickets should only be processed when the
requester's identity and ownership of the record
have been sufficiently verified.

Uncertain identity matches must be sent for human review.

POLICY 5: HUMAN APPROVAL

All proposed erasure and anonymisation actions must
be reviewed and approved before execution.

POLICY 6: VERIFICATION

After execution, the system must search the relevant
data stores again and record whether each approved
operation succeeded.
